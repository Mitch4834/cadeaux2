  • ✓ Explore the gifts selected by the organizer.
  • ✓ Reserve the gift they would like to give.
  • ✓ See gifts that have already been reserved, depending on the event settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Would You Like to Know More?

What types of events can I organize?

You can organize weddings, engagements, baby showers, birthdays, baptisms, graduations, housewarmings and other private celebrations.

How can my guests confirm their attendance?

They can access your event using the invitation link and indicate whether they will attend or be unable to make it.

How does gift reservation work?

You add gifts to your list. Guests can browse the available gifts and reserve the one they would like to give.

Can I track gift reservations and attendance confirmations?

Yes. Your organizer space allows you to view available information about your guests, attendance confirmations and gift reservations.

Do my guests need to install an app?

No. The platform is accessible through a web browser on a phone, tablet or computer.
