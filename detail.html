About

Get married in urban wine country!

City Winery is a premier wedding and event venue located in the Strip District, near Downtown Pittsburgh. A former produce terminal transformed into a contemporary, flexible and full-service event space.

With its industrial atmosphere, beautiful architecture, built-in stage, professional audiovisual equipment, private bars and elegant lighting, this venue offers a unique setting for weddings and celebrations.

The team works with each couple to customize their event. On-site catering, custom menus, tables, chairs, dinnerware and premium beverages are available.

City Winery can also host engagement parties, bridal showers, rehearsal dinners, welcome parties, anniversaries and other private celebrations.
